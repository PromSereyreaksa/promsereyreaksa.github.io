## Overview

Motify is an AI-powered video generator built for SaaS marketing, product launches, and promotional content. Product managers and marketers describe what they want in plain language, and Motify turns it into a high-quality, professional video: ads, feature announcements, and product explainers, with no video editing experience required.

## Problem

Polished motion graphics have always had high creative and financial barriers. Teams either learn complex tools like Adobe After Effects, pay an agency, or spend weeks iterating with motion designers. On top of that, keeping a consistent visual identity across AI-generated content is usually difficult, so results drift off-brand from one scene to the next.

## Approach

Motify takes a prompt-to-video approach: you describe the video, and it generates the result fast. Its Brand DNA system locks in a company's custom assets, colors, typography, voice, and target audience, and automatically enforces them across every generated scene, so each video looks like it came from the same team.

## Result

Motify makes motion graphics easy, fast, and cheap. Teams go from an idea to an on-brand video in minutes instead of weeks, without hiring an agency or learning a professional editor.
