## Overview

Motionly is an open-source AI-native motion graphics editor built to keep generated motion editable after the prompt is done.

## Problem

Most AI motion tools produce output that is hard to inspect, tweak, or reuse. Motionly was built to keep the speed of generation without losing editor control.

## Approach

The editor pairs a canvas, timeline, and reusable components with a readable `.motion` source format. That makes each project easier to refine, share, and export.

## Result

Motionly turns motion graphics into something you can continue editing instead of treating as a dead export.
