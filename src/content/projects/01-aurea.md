## Overview

AUREA is a portfolio builder for designers who want a public site without spending days on setup.

## Problem

Most portfolio tools are either too generic or too rigid. Designers need something in between.

## Approach

I focused on the repeat tasks that slow people down:

- setting visual rhythm and spacing
- making work pages feel consistent
- handling responsive layout details
- shipping a public site without fighting the stack

## Result

The product now gives designers a faster path from draft to published portfolio, with fewer choices to manage along the way.
